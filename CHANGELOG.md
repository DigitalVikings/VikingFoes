# Changelog

All notable changes to **VikingFoes** will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- **Build & Packaging Automation**: Resolved version string escaping in deployment and packaging toolchain.
- **Documentation & Compendium**: Clarified anatomical damage profiles and poise stagger thresholds across creature tiers.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- **Anatomical Damage Resistances**: Physical resistances tailored to creature physiology, penalizing mismatched attacks while rewarding elemental counter-play.
- **Physical Attack Type Conversions**: Creature attacks deliver accurate physical damage types (Boar slashing tusks, Brute blunt strikes, Serpent piercing bites).
- **Poise Stagger Ratings**: Creatures have calibrated poise pools governing stagger resistance, rewarding heavy blunt strikes and timed parries.
- **Organic Bleed Afflictions**: Predator attacks naturally inflict lacerating bleeds scaling with target armor through VikingLib's status effect pipeline.
